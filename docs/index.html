Campo Boys Soccer Calendars

Subscribe once and Campolindo's boys soccer games appear in your phone's calendar. Updated every day around noon from the school athletics site, so time changes and new games show up on their own.

How to subscribe

  1. iPhone, iPad or Mac: tap Apple Calendar under your player's team, then tap Subscribe.
  2. Google Calendar: tap Google Calendar, then Add. On Android, do this in a web browser once; the games then show in the Google Calendar app.
  3. Outlook or anything else: tap Copy link and add it as a calendar "from the internet" or "by URL".

Google Calendar checks subscribed calendars on its own timetable, usually every 12–24 hours, so a change can take up to a day to appear there. Apple devices refresh more often. Times are from the school site; confirm last-minute changes with the coach.
